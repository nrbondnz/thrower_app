## Project Overview

**Thrower App**: a Flutter app for iOS and Android. A static phone camera watches a wooden 5-ring board (5 for the inner ring down to 1 for the outer) that the user throws knives or axes at. The app detects the target and scores each throw. Decisions are in `docs/thrower/Architecture/Design Decisions.md`. Paused: **Entry point** (`docs/thrower/stories/story-checkpoint-entry-point.md`, waiting for closer footage). Last: **Quick draw** (`docs/thrower/stories/archive/story-checkpoint-quick-draw.md`): two throwers on a countdown, boards tapped, every frame timed from "go", the first knife to stick wins, on a recording of a real duel. Built 2026-10-16, device checks to come: **Players and targets** (`docs/thrower/stories/story-checkpoint-players-and-targets.md`): Play for 1 or 2 players, on 1 target (turns) or 2 (at once); and built 2026-10-17, device checks to come: **Game history** (`docs/thrower/stories/story-checkpoint-game-history.md`): finished games kept on the phone, Home → History; and **Score corrections** (`docs/thrower/stories/story-checkpoint-score-corrections.md`): tap a score on Play to change it, or an empty slot to add a missed throw; and built 2026-10-19, device checks to come: **Quick draw match** (`docs/thrower/stories/story-checkpoint-quick-draw-match.md`): the live Quick draw as three rounds, the score breaking a same-frame tie (Nigel's brother's rules), and the scoring named KATTA; and **Named players** (`docs/thrower/stories/story-checkpoint-named-players.md`): a name for each player on Play's setup screen, kept on the phone and used by Play, History and Quick draw. Accounts are planned in stages: `docs/thrower/stories/accounts/Accounts Roadmap.md` (stage 1, named players, built; stage 2 next).
