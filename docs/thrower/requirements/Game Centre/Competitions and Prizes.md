# Competitions and Prizes

Part of [[Requirements Index]] → Game Centre. **Status:** 💭 Idea, to be thought through

## What's Wanted
Winning competitions earns prizes. Nigel's note (2026-10-19): **"prize would eventually be cash but initially weekly top rank gets a month extra subscription for free"**, and a note in a **hall of fame**.

## What Exists Today
- Nothing yet. Needs [[Rankings]] and a [[Subscriptions]] scheme first.

### Hall of Fame
- Each week's winner noted in a hall of fame. To be defined.

## Open Questions
- **Hall of fame:** in the app for everyone to see; what it shows (name, week, score, a clip?).
- **Ties** at the top of a week.
- **Cash prizes (later):** UK rules on prize competitions and skill games need checking before any money or paid entry is involved, as do the App Store and Google Play rules on contests (official rules, the developer as sponsor, eligibility). Get advice before this stage.
- **Free month as a prize:** granted how? Apple and Google offer codes or promotional offers for subscriptions; this decides part of the payments design.
- A winner still in their 14 free days ([[Subscriptions]]): add the free month on after them?
- Only subscribers rank, so only subscribers win?
- Who can enter: age (knife throwing: adults only?), countries.
