# One Target

Part of [[Requirements Index]] → Basic play → [[Round Play]]. **Status:** ✅ Built (device checks to come where noted)

## What's Wanted
One or two players share one target.

## What Exists Today
- **1 player:** rounds of 3 throws on one board.
- **2 players, 1 target:** they **take turns**: one throws their 3 knives, the knives are collected, then the other. **Player 1 starts every round.** The panel says whose turn it is in large type (by name if the players are named: "Jo's turn"; [[Player Names]]), readable from the throwing line, and "Collect your knives" between turns.
- **Round done** (2026-10-23): once a turn has its 3 throws and nothing is collected (e.g. three bounce-outs), tap **Round done** to pass the turn ([[Round Play]]; device check RD2).
- Equal totals are a draw. Built 2026-10-16 ([[story-checkpoint-players-and-targets]] Task 2; device checks M0–M9 to come).
