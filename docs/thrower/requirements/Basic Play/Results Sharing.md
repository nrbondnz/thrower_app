# Results Sharing

Part of [[Requirements Index]] → Basic play. **Status:** 🗺️ Planned, not built

## What's Wanted
Results can be shared, by **all players or each player**.

## What Exists Today
- Nothing yet: History stays on the phone.
- Planned: [[story-checkpoint-record-and-share]] shares recordings, with the scoring drawn on, through the phone's **share sheet** (backlog #11).
- [[Accounts Roadmap]]: stage 3 (games played on the host's phone appear in each linked friend's own history) and stage 5 (each thrower gets their own clips and shares them as they choose).

- **Players without the app** get their clips through the **share sheet** (Nigel, 2026-10-19); **friends** (with the app) get them in their own account. See [[Accounts Roadmap]] → App Users, Players and Friends.

## Open Questions

- What is shared: a picture of the score card, a video with overlays, a link to a results page?
- Where to: the share sheet (any app), or posting straight to named services?
