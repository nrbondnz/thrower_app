# Two Targets

Part of [[Requirements Index]] → Basic play → [[Round Play]]. **Status:** ✅ Built (device checks to come where noted)

## What's Wanted
Two players, a target each, throwing at the same time, seen by one phone.

## What Exists Today
- The phone stands behind the throwers and sees both boards. Tap each board's centre (Player 1, then Player 2; by name if named: [[Player Names]]) and lock them; each board is watched on its own crop of the picture and **scores for its own player**.
- Both throw their round; knives are collected **together**, when both have finished. A player who finishes first is told to wait. **Round done** (2026-10-23) shows only once both have finished the round, for a round with nothing to collect ([[Round Play]]; device check RD3).
- Built 2026-10-16 ([[story-checkpoint-players-and-targets]] Task 3; device checks M0–M9 to come). Quick draw uses the same two-board setup.
- **Each board's outline can be dragged into place** once both are found (move it, turn and stretch it), only when no one is throwing. **Adjust boards** unlocks them later, keeping both; on two-target Play it stops watching and clears the marks, and the scores stay. Built 2026-10-19 ([[story-checkpoint-adjust-boards]]; device checks to come).
- **The Instructions explain it** (*Two players on two targets*): drag an outline into place, or tap Choose boards again; if a board or the phone is knocked, tap **Adjust boards** when no one is throwing, drag the outline back, then **Lock boards**. Watching stops until you lock again; the scores stay (2026-10-19, Task 2).
