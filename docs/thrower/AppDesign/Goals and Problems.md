# Goals and Problems

Part of [[App Design Index]]. Requirement: [[Look and Feel]].

## What the App Already Does

- Detects real knife and axe throws.
- Finds where each one hit the target.
- Works out the score automatically.
- Supports competition (two throwers, Quick draw, the Game Center leaderboard) and keeps history.

The look works, but it feels like an internal utility or a testing tool, not a premium sports app.

## The Aim

The redesign should make the app feel like:

> A professional AI-powered throwing sports platform.

Not:

> A camera calibration utility.

## Problems With the Current Look

### 1. Too much empty space
Home is a column of buttons in the middle of a blank screen. It feels sparse and unfinished.

### 2. Every function has equal weight
Play and Calibrate target look equally important. Someone opening the app should know at once what the main thing to do is.

### 3. Technical tools mixed with what throwers want
Throwers care about:
- throwing and scoring;
- their history and how they're improving;
- competing.

They don't care about:
- calibration;
- the camera test (Live test);
- the scoring test target.

Those belong together, out of the way ([[Navigation and Structure]] → Setup).

### 4. No visual identity
Nothing says knife throwing, axe throwing, competition, scoring, accuracy or targets. See [[Brand and Voice]] and [[Colour and Themes]].

### 5. The main feature isn't shown
The app's big feature is that **the camera sees each throw and scores it**. Nothing on Home says so. See [[Home Screen]] → Hero.
