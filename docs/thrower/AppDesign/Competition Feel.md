# Competition Feel

Part of [[App Design Index]]. **Later**: not in [[story-checkpoint-app-design]].

Borrowed from archery and shooting:
- achievement badges;
- personal bests;
- accuracy records;
- streaks (sessions in a row, weeks in a row);
- weekly stats, with the weekly Game Center leaderboard ([[Rankings]]).

## Personal Best

At the end of a session that beats the best:

```text
New personal best
312 points
Previous best: 305
```

- "Best" needs to be like for like: the same kind of session and length (or Ranked, always 5 × 3).
- Whose best: a named thrower's. Two names on one phone would each have their own.
