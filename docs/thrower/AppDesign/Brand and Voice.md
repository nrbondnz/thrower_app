# Brand and Voice

Part of [[App Design Index]]. **Status:** THROWER, the tagline and START THROWING built 2026-10-23 (Task 4). Settled: [[dont-ask-again]] → Throwing and sessions, not games.

## Positioning

The app should feel:
- professional;
- competitive;
- technical;
- accurate;
- fast.

Think **"Strava for throwing sports"**, not "the local axe throwing venue".

## Inspiration

Model it on apps whose flow is the same as ours (a picture, then detection, then a score, then analytics), not on venue websites:
- archery scoring apps (ArcheryAI, Archery Score Tracker);
- shooting analytics apps (ShotCoach, AimSight);
- sports performance trackers (Strava).

## The Name

- In the app's hero: **THROWER** (Nigel, 2026-10-23), bold, in capitals.
- The name under the phone's app icon is unchanged.
- The website is knifeandaxethrower.com.

## How the App Talks

Nigel, 2026-10-23: throwers don't think of themselves as playing a game.

- The main button says **Start throwing**, not "Start match" or "Play".
- New words talk about **throwing**, **sessions**, **rounds** and **knives**: "Last session", "Knives thrown".
- The type of session is still chosen under **Which game?** for now (Scoring, Precision, Three targets, Ranked). Changing those words, and "Game over", is a later pass, so the Instructions and the device checks stay right.
- Short and plain. Numbers first: "287 points", not "You scored 287 points in your last game".

## Taglines

The hero's line under THROWER:
- **"Every throw seen. Every score counted."** (in use)
- Others considered: "Real-time AI scoring for knife and axe throwing"; "AI target detection, competition scoring".
