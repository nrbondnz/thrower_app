## Duel Test Case (Original Video, 2:42)

Worked out 2026-10-14 from `reference/live-camera/1.mp4` and `2.mp4`: two crops of the original, same timeline, 60 fps, same audio. Mark counted down "three, two, one, go" (barely audible). Both threw; each board's round 4/5 has only this throw (answer key `b2-t10`, `b1-t13`).

- **Knife lands (video)**
  - **Mark: board 2 (right):** 162.12–162.13 s: in flight 162.083–162.117 s from the top right
  - **The Captain: board 1 (left):** 162.15–162.17 s: first seen above the bull at 162.150 s
- **Sound (audio)**
  - **Mark: board 2 (right):** **162.132 s**: one sharp onset from silence
  - **The Captain: board 1 (left):** not separable: the sound rattles on for ~70 ms with peaks at .136, .154 and .174 s and never drops back
- **Result**
  - **Mark: board 2 (right):** **5**, top of the bull
  - **The Captain: board 1 (left):** **bounce-out**: hits the bull, slides off down the board (it lands below at about 162.55 s, a later thud)

- **Winner: Mark, by 1–2 frames (about 17–33 ms) on the video**, and on score. The audio's single onset at 162.132 s fits Mark's landing, but the Captain's impact can't be picked out of the rattle that follows, so the margin rests on the video. (Corrected 2026-10-14: an earlier reading of "two thuds 30 ms apart" was an artefact of the onset detector's 30 ms minimum gap.)
- **Decoy:** board 1 brightens across its whole face at about 162.0 s (light or exposure flicker, not a knife). A plain frame-difference trigger takes it for the throw and gets the order wrong; this test should catch that.
- **The knife already in board 1** at the right of the bull (3 o'clock) is from an earlier throw and doesn't move.
- **"Go": 161.3 s** (Nigel, by ear, 2026-10-14), so both throws took about 0.8 s from go to landing. (Earlier: unconfirmed, a speech-band burst at about 160.9–161.4 s.)
- **Now a test:** this pair is the Quick draw clip (`assets/live/quickdraw.tfrm`, 158.5–166 s; answer key `reference/live-camera/quickdraw.json`). `test/game/quick_draw_replay_test.dart` checks Mark stuck 5 and the Captain bounced, each impact within a frame of the video landing frames (217 and 219 in the clip), Mark first. Impact is the frame with the largest change on the board's face, which lands on the knife, not on the 162.0 s brightening (2026-10-15).
- **Method** (to turn into a test): per-board frame difference restricted to the target (with a background strip to reject whole-frame changes), plus a 2 ms audio envelope. Count an onset only where the sound **rises from near-silence**, not on a fixed minimum gap (that splits one long sound into fake "thuds"). Then match onsets to boards by the video landing frames.
