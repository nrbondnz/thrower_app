Game flow

Choosing two boards by tapping (Quick draw, two-target Play)

Tap the centre of Player 1's board, then Player 2's (by name, if the players are named), drag an outline into place if needed, then Lock boards. Both Quick draw screens and two-target Play do it the same way; Quick draw can draw round the boards instead.

There may be more boards in the picture than players, so the user picks them. Finding boards automatically missed board 2 on half the frames of the duel clip.

Once both are found, each outline can be dragged into place (only when no one is throwing: the phone stands behind the throwers). Adjust boards unlocks them later, keeping both; on two-target Play it stops watching and clears the marks, and the scores stay (2026-10-19).

Quick draw only (2026-10-21): Draw round the boards. A finger drawn round each board on the screen (LoopDrawer) becomes the board: the ellipse fitted to the loop (areaBoard), or four corners if it was drawn round a square or rectangular board, no ring search. Any knife that sticks inside counts; only the time decides, so the same frame is a drawn round. Then drag, Lock and Adjust boards as for tapped boards.
