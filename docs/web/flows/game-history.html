Game flow

Keeping and viewing finished games

When a game of Play finishes it is kept on the phone, once. The History tab lists the games under the stats; a game shows every round.

Saving never holds up play: the record is handed to the history and Play carries on. If the save fails, Play says so in a snack bar.

Finish game ends a game early: a round in progress counts, and two players are judged on the rounds both have played. History shows "2 of 9 rounds" and marks a round that didn't count.

Only finished games are kept. A last-round knife that falls out after the end saves the same game again with the new score, under the time it first finished.

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING, the last session, and the Throwing types cards), Leaderboards (in place of Throw, later on 2026-10-23), History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, Help and support (the Instructions and the website's policy pages), Appearance, the Live test recordings, Calibrate target and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.
