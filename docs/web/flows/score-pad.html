Game flow

Precision and Three targets: the score pad

Two more games on Play, chosen under Which game?, scored by hand for now: each knife's score is tapped on a pad. The game type changes only where knives are thrown from or at; the turns, rounds, corrections and History are Play's own.

Precision: 7 rounds of 3, each from its own distance (3, 5, 7, 3, 4, 5, 7 m, Nigel's guess, to confirm). Three targets: three boards, one knife at each per round, 3, 5 or 7 rounds. Two players take turns.

Scoring by hand brings someone to the phone after every knife: the screen says to score when no one is throwing, or to have someone who isn't throwing keep score.

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING, the last session, and the Throwing types cards), Leaderboards (in place of Throw, later on 2026-10-23), History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, Help and support (the Instructions and the website's policy pages), Appearance, Calibrate target and, in test builds only (2026-10-23), the Live test recordings and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.
