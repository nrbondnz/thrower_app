Game flow

Ranked game: posting a score to Game Center

Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, in strict mode; at the end of a full game, on the iPhone, Post score sends the total once to a Game Center leaderboard that restarts each month.

Strict mode (Nigel: scores can only be changed "to mark a bounce out but not to change scores to higher"): the rules themselves refuse anything but 0 by hand, and the popup offers only 0. Finish game early, and the game can no longer be posted.

3 m is the thrower's word: the setup screen asks for it before Start. Nothing goes to Game Center unless the player taps Post score.

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING and the last session), Throw, History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, the Instructions, Appearance, the Live test recordings, Calibrate target and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.
