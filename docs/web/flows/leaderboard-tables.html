Game flow

Leaderboard tables: this week and last week

From the Leaderboards tab or View leaderboard, on the iPhone: this week's and last week's tables of the Game Center leaderboard, top 25 by nickname, with your own place.

Backlog #22 (Nigel, 2026-10-22: "a best 3 meter score table refreshed each month, previous month results table"). The Game Center leaderboard was set up weekly, so the tables are weekly. No all-time table for now.

The app's own iPhone code loads both weeks: the games_services plugin gives only your own entry for last week, and loads every player's photo. Only names and scores come back, plus when the week began, which can confirm rankedLeaderboardStart.

In a recurring leaderboard's first week there's no last week: the table says "No results for last week yet".

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING, the last session, and the Throwing types cards), Leaderboards (in place of Throw, later on 2026-10-23), History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, Help and support (the Instructions and the website's policy pages), Appearance, Calibrate target and, in test builds only (2026-10-23), the Live test recordings and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.
