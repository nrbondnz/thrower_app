Game flow

Quick draw on a recording: every frame, then the winner

Live test → Quick draw: a recording of a real duel. Each board is watched on its own crop at every frame from 1 s before go; each knife is timed from go; the first to stick wins.

The picture and sound play at their own pace and may skip frames on a slow phone. Analysis gets its own copy of every frame, in order, so the result can come a little after the picture, but no frame is missed.

On the recording of the duel, Player 2 (Mark) wins: stuck 5, about 0.82 s after go.

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING, the last session, and the Throwing types cards), Leaderboards (in place of Throw, later on 2026-10-23), History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, the Instructions, Appearance, the Live test recordings, Calibrate target and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.

judgeQuickDraw and QuickDrawMatch

A round's winner: the first knife to stick. A bounce-out or a false start loses; on the same frame the higher score wins, the same score is a draw; if nobody sticks, there's no winner. The live Quick draw is a match of three rounds.
