Game flow

Live Quick draw: start signal to result

Home → Quick draw: lock the boards, a starter taps Start, 5 beeps and lights, a random pause, the go beep, then the result in large type with a chime.

Nobody touches the phone between Start and the result, and the result is large enough to read from the throwing line, so no one walks up to a phone beside two live lanes. Neither the chime nor the result means the lanes are clear.

The camera can't be asked for a frame again, so each frame's board crops are queued and analysed in order. A slow phone reports late; past 75 MB a board, frames are dropped and the result says so.

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING and the last session), Throw, History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, the Instructions, Appearance, the Live test recordings, Calibrate target and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.

judgeQuickDraw and QuickDrawMatch
