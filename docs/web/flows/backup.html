Game flow

Backing up: the sign-in merge and each kept game

Signed in, the kept games and player names are backed up to the user's own rows, and come back on a new phone. The phone stays the source of truth.

Offline-first: every change is made on the phone first. Signed in, it also goes to the backup, without waiting; anything that couldn't be sent (no signal) is caught up by the next merge, on sign-in or with Back up now.

The merge is by game id: games only in the backup come to the phone, games only on the phone go up. Delete and Clear history reach the backup too. Known limit: a game deleted while signed out comes back at the next sync.

Rows are owner-only (allow.owner()): KeptGame (id = <sub>:<gameId>, the record as the app's own JSON) and PlayerNameList (id = the sub). Delete account erases them, then the account.

UI

Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING, the last session, and the Throwing types cards), Leaderboards (in place of Throw, later on 2026-10-23), History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, Help and support (the Instructions and the website's policy pages), Appearance, Calibrate target and, in test builds only (2026-10-23), the Live test recordings and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.
