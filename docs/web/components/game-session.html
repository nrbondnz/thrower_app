GameSession and Match

A game of 3, 5 or 9 rounds of 3 throws per player. A board visit (collecting the knives) closes the round.

Play, on each throw event

A bounce-out scores 0. A knife that sticks and then falls out before it is collected turns that throw into 0. Once the knives are collected, Play shows the next round straight away (backlog #12).

Match holds a GameSession per player (2026-10-16). Two players on one target take turns: 3 knives, collect, then the other player, Player 1 starting every round. On two targets each board scores for its own player. The higher total wins; equal totals are a draw.
