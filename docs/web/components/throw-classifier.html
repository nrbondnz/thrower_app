ThrowTracker and classifyThrow

Decides what an episode was: a new knife stuck, a bounce-out, a known knife falling out, or someone visiting the board.

Type

Defined in
lib/game/throw_tracker.dart

Used when
Each finished motion episode

It is a board visit if someone stood there, if 10% or more of the region changed at once, or if one shape covering 3% or more of the region moved. People are 4% and up; knives are up to 2.2%. A visit closes the round, and the known knives are forgotten.

findNewObject looks for a connected changed shape touching the board (its shape: the outer ring, or a drawn board's four corners), and checks it against the knives already in the board, so a knife falling out isn't taken for a new one.
