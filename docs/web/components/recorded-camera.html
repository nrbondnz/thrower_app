RecordedCamera

A Live test recording played as a camera: a FramePack of JPEG frames, in real time, with its sound.

Used when
Live test

Frames are decoded by Flutter's native image codec, off the UI thread. A phone that falls behind skips to the newest frame, so the picture keeps pace with the sound.

The recordings are in assets/live/: Target 1 and Target 2 (322 px, 15 fps), and the Quick draw clip (720 × 1280, 60 fps, with quickdraw.m4a). Recording.go is when the clip's countdown says go (2.8 s).
