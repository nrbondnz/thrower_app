Entry point

Where the blade goes into the wood, which is what is scored. Not the handle, and not the shadow.

Type
Functions
Defined in
lib/vision/entry_point.dart
Used when
A stuck knife

Seen face on, faceOnEntry takes the knife's shape nearest the bull. From the side, GeometricEntryEstimator compares the before and after pictures by colour, keeps the grey steel pixels (a shadow keeps the board's hue), finds the knife's axis, and takes the end away from the outward direction.

Nigel's rule: "find the solid shape of the knife; score where the blade goes into the wood; a shadow comes from the knife, on the side away from the light". Improving this is the paused entry-point story.
