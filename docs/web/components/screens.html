Screens

Since 2026-10-23 the app opens on four tabs (AppShell): Home (START THROWING, the last session, and the Throwing types cards), Leaderboards (in place of Throw, later on 2026-10-23), History and Setup. They lead to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players; Scoring by the camera, or Precision and Three targets scored by hand; Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m, one player, postable to Game Center on the iPhone), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), and, under Setup, Help and support (the Instructions and the website's policy pages), Appearance, the Live test recordings, Calibrate target and the Scoring test target; and, in a build with accounts, the Account screen.

Used when
The user, from the tabs (Home, Leaderboards, History, Setup)

The look (2026-10-23, the app-design story): light and dark themes from AppTheme, chosen by Setup → Appearance; the camera screens are wrapped in CameraTheme, always dark. The shared pieces (ActionCard, StatTile, TargetMark, SectionHeading) are in lib/ui/theme/.

Help and support (2026-10-23, the compliance story): Setup links the website's Support, Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages and the support email, opened outside the app with url_launcher (linkOpenerProvider, swapped in tests). Delete account opens the Account screen in a build with accounts; otherwise NoAccountScreen says there's no account on this phone and links the website's Delete Your Account page. The policies live on the website, not in the app.

Screens only draw and react: they read every other layer through Riverpod providers, so tests swap any layer for a fake.

CameraView shows a camera preview and handles a refused permission, the app going to the background and re-opening. Both Quick draw screens choose boards the same way, through quick_draw_boards.dart: BoardChoosing (tap → find → outline), BoardsOverlay and BoardsPainter. Two-target Play does too. Once both boards are found, BoardsEditor lets each outline be dragged into place, with the single target's drag rules (grabAt, dragged); Adjust boards unlocks them later, keeping both (2026-10-19).

Quick draw's boards can instead be drawn round by finger (2026-10-21, for boards whose rings the app can't find): LoopDrawer draws the path, and the ellipse fitted to the loop (areaBoard), or its four corners for a square or rectangular board, is the board, timed only, never scored. Two-target Play never offers it: it needs scores.

Players can be named (2026-10-19): on the setup screen, or with Names on Quick draw before Start. A blank seat is Player 1 / Player 2. Play, History and Quick draw all show the names; Quick draw's per-player result lines keep "1:" / "2:", matching the board numbers on the picture.

On the recording's Quick draw, Play shows only once the boards are locked: played unlocked, nothing would be analysed (2026-10-16).
