Screens

Home leads to Play (rounds of 3, for 1 or 2 players), the History of finished games, Quick draw (two throwers on a start signal), the Instructions, the Live test recordings, Calibrate target and the Scoring test target.

Used when
The user, from Home

Screens only draw and react: they read every other layer through Riverpod providers, so tests swap any layer for a fake.

CameraView shows a camera preview and handles a refused permission, the app going to the background and re-opening. Both Quick draw screens choose boards the same way, through quick_draw_boards.dart: BoardChoosing (tap → find → outline), BoardsOverlay and BoardsPainter.

On the recording's Quick draw, Play shows only once the boards are locked: played unlocked, nothing would be analysed (2026-10-16).
