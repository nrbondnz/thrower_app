Screens

Used when
The user, from Home

Screens only draw and react: they read every other layer through Riverpod providers, so tests swap any layer for a fake.

CameraView shows a camera preview and handles a refused permission, the app going to the background and re-opening. Both Quick draw screens choose boards the same way, through quick_draw_boards.dart: BoardChoosing (tap → find → outline), BoardsOverlay and BoardsPainter. Two-target Play does too. Once both boards are found, BoardsEditor lets each outline be dragged into place, with the single target's drag rules (grabAt, dragged); Adjust boards unlocks them later, keeping both (2026-10-19).

Quick draw's boards can instead be drawn round by finger (2026-10-21, for boards whose rings the app can't find): LoopDrawer draws the path, and the ellipse fitted to the loop (areaBoard), or its four corners for a square or rectangular board, is the board, timed only, never scored. Two-target Play never offers it: it needs scores.

Players can be named (2026-10-19): on the setup screen, or with Names on Quick draw before Start. A blank seat is Player 1 / Player 2. Play, History and Quick draw all show the names; Quick draw's per-player result lines keep "1:" / "2:", matching the board numbers on the picture.

On the recording's Quick draw, Play shows only once the boards are locked: played unlocked, nothing would be analysed (2026-10-16).
