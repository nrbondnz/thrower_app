Camera · Component

LiveCamera

The phone's back camera through the camera plugin, at ResolutionPreset.high, with no audio.

Type
TargetCamera
Defined in
lib/camera/live_camera.dart
Used when
Calibrate, Play, the live Quick draw

Android frames arrive as YUV420 in the sensor's layout, usually sideways; iOS frames arrive as BGRA, already upright. uprightRotation works out the turn from the sensor and the phone's orientation.

The live Quick draw asks for 60 fps. If the camera won't open at that rate it is opened again at its default, but never after a refused permission. Frames are stamped when they reach Dart, not when they were captured (Known Issues).
