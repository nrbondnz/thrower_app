ThrowStats

The stats over the sessions kept on the phone (2026-10-23): sessions, knives, per knife, 5s, hit rate, and the best ranked total or best round.

Used when
History and Home, from the kept sessions

Every thrower's knives count together; sessions on a Live test recording don't count. The knife stats come only from sessions kept with each knife (from 2026-10-23); partial says when some were left out, and History says so.

Per knife (points ÷ knives) compares sessions of any length. Hit rate is knives that scored ÷ knives thrown. Best ranked is a full Ranked session's total.
