ThrowWatcher

Watches one board's frames and turns motion into throw events: stuck (with its entry point), bounce-out, fell out, or a board visit.

Type
Class
Defined in
lib/game/throw_watcher.dart
Used when
Play (one board); Quick draw (one per board)

Each frame becomes a ~320 px brightness image (frameToLuma) for the MotionDetector. While the board is still, a full-resolution "before" picture is kept, made in a background isolate.

Play looks at a frame at most every 60 ms (about 15 a second). Quick draw looks at every frame, as its timing needs them all.
