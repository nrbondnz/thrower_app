MotionDetector

Finds stretches of motion in the board's region (episodes): watching → motion → settling → settled, or blocked when something stands in front of the board.

Used when
Each frame ThrowWatcher looks at

A pixel counts as changed when it differs by more than 6 × the learned sensor noise, at least 12 levels. An episode ends once the board has been still for 0.3 s and looks like the board again.

Its impact is the frame with the largest moving shape on the target, when the board jolts and the knife stops. Quick draw times throws by it.
