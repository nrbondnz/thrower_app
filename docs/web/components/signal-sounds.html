The start signal and the result

They are small WAV files in assets/sounds/ (about 60 KB), made by tool/make_signal_sounds.dart: a 1 kHz beep, a longer 1.5 kHz go, and two rising notes for the result. Each is preloaded on its own player. They duck other apps' audio rather than stopping it, and play with the iPhone's silent switch on.
