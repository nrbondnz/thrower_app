## 2. Safety

Knife and axe throwing is dangerous and can cause serious injury. You are responsible for throwing safely and for following your venue's rules and the law where you live.

- **The App cannot tell whether the lane is clear.** Nothing in the App means it is safe to throw, or safe to approach the target.
- Place your phone **out of the line of throw**, to the side of the lane, and never stand in the lane to use it.
- Only go to the phone or the target when no one is throwing.
- Do not use the App to throw at anything other than a proper target.
