**Thrower** is made and operated by Bond Consultancy Ltd. This page explains how to delete some of your data, your backed-up games or player names, **without deleting your account**. To delete everything, see [Delete Your Account](/delete-account).

## Delete games in the App

While you are signed in:

- **One game:** tap **History** at the bottom of the screen, tap the game, then tap the bin ("Delete this game").
- **All games:** tap **History**, then **Clear history**, and confirm.

The games are deleted from your phone and from your backup. If you have no signal at that moment, a deleted game may come back from the backup the next time the App syncs; delete it again once you have a signal.

## Delete player names

Player names in your backup are replaced by the names you set in the App. To remove them from your backup completely, email us (below).

## Or ask us by email

Email [kaat-support@knifeandaxethrower.com](mailto:kaat-support@knifeandaxethrower.com) **from your account's email address**, with the subject line **"Data Deletion Request"**, and say what you want deleted (for example "all my backed-up games" or "my player names").

- We use the email address it comes from to find your account. We may reply to confirm, if anything is unclear.
- We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

## What is kept

Your account (your email address and sign-in) stays, so you can keep using the backup. Nothing else is kept from the data you asked us to delete.
