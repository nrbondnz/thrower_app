Need help with **Thrower**? Email us at [kaat-support@knifeandaxethrower.com](mailto:kaat-support@knifeandaxethrower.com). We aim to reply within a few working days.

It helps to tell us your phone model, whether it is an iPhone or Android, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

### The App doesn't find my target

Light the board evenly, and fit the whole board in the camera picture. The phone should be on a steady mount, to the side of the lane. You can drag the outline into place by hand before you lock it.

### A score is wrong

Tap the score on the Play screen to change it, or tap an empty slot to add a missed throw. Only do this when no one is throwing.

### Do I need an account?

No. Everything that scores works without one. An account backs up your games and player names, so they come back on another phone.

### How do I delete my account or data?

See [Delete Your Account](/delete-account) and [Delete Your Data](/delete-data).

## Safety

The App cannot tell whether the lane is clear. Keep your phone, and yourself, out of the line of throw, and only go to the phone or the target when no one is throwing. See our [Terms of Service](/terms).
