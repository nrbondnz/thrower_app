Need help with **Thrower**? Email us at [kaat-support@knifeandaxethrower.com](mailto:kaat-support@knifeandaxethrower.com). We aim to reply within a few working days.

It helps to tell us your phone model, whether it is an iPhone or Android, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

### The App doesn't find my target

Light the board evenly, and fit the whole board in the camera picture. The phone should be on a steady mount, to the side of the lane. You can drag the outline into place by hand before you lock it.

### A score is wrong

Tap the score on the Play screen to change it, or tap an empty slot to add a missed throw. Only do this when no one is throwing. In a ranked game, a score can only be changed to 0 (a bounce-out or a miss).

### How do I get on the leaderboard?

On an iPhone signed in to Game Center, play **Ranked: 5 × 3 at 3 m**, or a normal game of 5 rounds of 3 knives from 3 m with no score changed by hand. At the end, tap **Post score**. You can also post later from **History**, in the same leaderboard week. Tap **Leaderboards** at the bottom of the screen to see it. Leaderboards start again every week.

### History says "Cannot be posted"

Open the game in History to see why: for example, it had two throwers, a round had fewer than 3 knives, a score was changed by hand, or it was thrown in an earlier leaderboard week.

### Do I need an account?

No. Everything that scores works without one, and the leaderboards use Game Center, not an account. An account backs up your games and player names, so they come back on another phone.

### How do I delete my account or data?

See [Delete Your Account](/delete-account) and [Delete Your Data](/delete-data).

## Safety

The App cannot tell whether the lane is clear. Keep your phone, and yourself, out of the line of throw, and only go to the phone or the target when no one is throwing. See our [Terms of Service](/terms).
